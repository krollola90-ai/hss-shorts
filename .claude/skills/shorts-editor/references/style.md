# Style rules (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

## Frame

- 1080×1920, 30 fps, ≤ 120 s.
- Platform UI covers the top 168 px and everything below ~1330 px, plus a ~140 px right rail. Text lives inside 168–1330 px vertically. Captions sit at their bottom edge (≈ y 1280).
- Never cover the speaker's face with a card: overlays go above the face (`top`) for a normal selfie framing; check every overlay on the contact sheet.

## Hook (0:00–0:05)

- Frame 0 is the thumbnail: face plus the hook text already on screen. No black, no logo.
- Cut the first sentence to its punchiest form (use overrides to drop throat-clearing).
- Hook block on screen from ~0.05 s, heavy punch-in (1.2–1.25), one big hit SFX.
- If the speaker never says a hook line, write a text hook that matches what follows. Don't promise what the video doesn't deliver.

## Body

- Numbered segments get a `badge` on the word "number"/"first"/"второй"… with a `pop`.
- Numbers and stats get a `stat` card and caption `emphasis`.
- Tools, sites, news → real screenshot B-roll (`image`), `camera-shutter` or `whoosh`.
- Prompts, commands, code → `code` card with `typing`.
- Hard cuts on speech only; no crossfades on the talking head.
- Zoom pattern: alternate in/out; never zoom during a full-screen B-roll.

## Sound

- Voice is king: mastered to −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dB.
- Music bed −22 dB, starts with the video, fades out over the last 1.2 s.
- SFX at −9 dB by default; impact hits can go to −6. The hit lands on the visual event (the library stores each file's peak offset).
- Mapping: whoosh = zoom/slide/scene change; pop/click = text, badge, logo; vine-boom/bass-drop = hook or shocking fact; typing/ding = code, prompt, notification; riser = build-up into a reveal (ends on the reveal); record-scratch = "but wait"; ka-ching = money; correct/wrong = myth vs fact; bonk/fahhh/bruh = comedic fails (sparingly).

## Captions

- Word-by-word karaoke, 3 words per group, broken at punctuation and 150 ms pauses.
- Uppercase (Cyrillic too); the style font (default Inter Black). The active word turns the style accent colour; emphasised words get a solid plate (`emphasis_style: plate`).
- Captions are in the spoken language; don't translate unless asked.
